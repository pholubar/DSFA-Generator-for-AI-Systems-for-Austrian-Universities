Austrian University DPIA Generator for AI Systems

Structured DPIA generation and review framework for AI systems in Austrian universities, integrating GDPR, the EU AI Act, research governance, and Austrian labour law.

⸻

Overview

The Austrian University DPIA (DSFA) Generator is an open framework for the structured creation, review, and improvement of Data Protection Impact Assessments (DPIAs) for AI systems used in higher education and research environments.

The framework was developed in response to the rapid adoption of generative AI, AI assistants, agentic systems, and AI-enabled platforms in universities, where data protection, academic freedom, research security, employee rights, and AI governance increasingly intersect.

Unlike generic DPIA templates, this framework explicitly addresses the requirements of Austrian universities and research institutions.

⸻

Key Features

* Structured 13-section DPIA model
* GDPR-compliant DPIA workflow (Art. 35 GDPR)
* AI-specific risk catalogue
* Explicit treatment of AI governance issues
* Austrian university governance perspective
* Works council (ArbVG) assessment module
* Schrems II and third-country transfer review
* Research freedom and research security considerations
* AI Act alignment and governance assessment
* Review mode for existing DPIAs
* Transparent handling of uncertainty and source quality

⸻

Scope

The framework supports:

* Creation of new DPIA drafts
* Review and gap analysis of existing DPIAs
* Risk assessment for AI systems
* Governance assessments for AI deployments
* Preparation of institutional decision-making processes

The framework does not:

* provide legal advice
* replace the responsibility of the Data Protection Officer
* automatically approve systems
* determine legal compliance

Final responsibility remains with the competent bodies of the respective institution.

Legal and Governance Framework

The framework draws upon:

* General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR)
* Austrian Data Protection Act (DSG)
* Austrian DPIA Regulations
* Austrian Labour Constitution Act (ArbVG)
* EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)
* EDPB Guidelines and Recommendations
* University governance and research governance principles
